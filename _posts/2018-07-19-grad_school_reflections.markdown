## Some thoughts about the first year of grad school

Meeting Mahdi Soltanolkotabi was so cool! Having all my courses taught by him in my first semester is something I'll always remember with joy and gratitude. He is so funny and knowledgable. I loved how  he taught us how his advisor, Emmanuel Candes thinks and approaches research, always having complaints and things to do constructive criticism and improve. He made so manny jokes I kept laughing all his classes, like 'you know there's this other school in LA...' Or making jokes about drinking a shot every time the screen projection did not work (which happened quite often).


Taking classes with Stephen Tu has also been a great pleasure and I've been impressed by how sharp his mathematical reasonsing is, I've never seen someone with so much depth and breadth in so many topics ranging from statistics to optimization, dynamical systems, optimal control, robotics, or pretty much any other CS topic, he always had something thoughtful to say, yet he always remained humble and curious.
