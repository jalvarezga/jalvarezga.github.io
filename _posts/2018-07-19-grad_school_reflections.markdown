## Some thoughts about the first year of grad school

As I'm finishing my first year and starting my second, I thought it would be nice to pause and write some words, becasue time has gone by at an incredibly fast pace. 

Meeting Mahdi Soltanolkotabi was so cool! Having all my courses taught by him in my first semester is something I'll always remember with joy and gratitude. He is so funny and knowledgeable. I loved how  he taught us emphasizing the syle of  Emmanuel Candes and his approach to research, always having complaints and things to do constructive criticism and improvements. He made so manny jokes I kept laughing all his classes. The kind of jokes that nobody laughs, but I literally had to bite my tongue to control my laugh. Comments like 'you know there's this other school in LA...' Or making jokes about drinking a shot every time the screen projector did not work (which happened quite often). I loved how he emphasized the power and beauty of simple math towards obtaining meaningful theorems. His sense of humor is just amazing.


Taking classes with Stephen Tu has also been a great pleasure. I was always in awe by how sharp his mathematical reasonsing is, I've never seen someone with so much depth and breadth in so many topics ranging from statistics to optimization, dynamical systems, optimal control, robotics, or pretty much any other CS topic. He always had something thoughtful to say, yet he always remained humble and curious to learn. That same semester I also took mixed-integer optimization with Bistra Dilkina and Andres Gomes. I really enjoyed that class as well, they are very nice porfessors. I was impressed by the fact that there's an entire research field dedicated to a single algorihtm, in this case the Branch and Bound algorithm. Maybe that's a dramatic claim, but maybe this also holds for things like conformal prediction or gradient descent, like literally simple algorithms that have established entire research fields, which is crazy to think about.
